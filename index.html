Brain Teasers

Brain Teasers is a trivia and puzzle application designed to provide quick and entertaining brain challenges, especially for Apple Watch users.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I start a quiz?

Open Brain Teasers on your Apple Watch and select the start option to begin answering questions.

How is my score calculated?

Your score is based on your answers and progress within the quiz.

Does Brain Teasers require an Internet connection?

The main quiz features can work locally on your device. Some system-related features may depend on your device configuration.

Bean Break Master

Bean Break Master is a casual puzzle game for iPhone and Apple Watch. Players complete bean-breaking puzzle challenges and progress through different levels.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I start the game?

Open Bean Break Master on your iPhone or Apple Watch and tap the start button to enter the game.

How do I progress through levels?

Complete the objective of the current puzzle to unlock or continue to the next level.

Is my game progress stored online?

Current game progress is primarily stored locally on your device.

Does Bean Break Master require an Internet connection?

The main gameplay does not require a continuous Internet connection.

Troubleshooting

What should I do if an app is not working correctly?

Please make sure your iPhone and Apple Watch are running supported system versions. Try closing and reopening the app, or restarting your device. If the problem continues, please contact us.

The Apple Watch app does not open correctly. What should I do?

Make sure your Apple Watch is properly paired with your iPhone, the application is installed on the watch, and both devices are running supported system versions.
