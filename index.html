Welcome to the official support page for Brain Teasers.

Brain Teasers is a fun trivia and puzzle app designed to provide quick and entertaining brain challenges on Apple Watch.

If you experience any problems while using the app, please contact us. We will be happy to assist you.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I start a quiz?

Open the app on your Apple Watch and select a quiz to begin.

How is my score calculated?

Your score is based on the number of questions you answer correctly.

Does the app require an Internet connection?

Some features may be available without an Internet connection. Availability may depend on the current version of the app.

What should I do if the app is not working correctly?

Please make sure that your Apple Watch and iPhone are running supported system versions. You can also try restarting the app. If the problem continues, please contact us.
