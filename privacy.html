1. Information We Collect / 我们收集的信息

Our applications do not require users to create an account, register, or log in in order to use their main features.

目前，上述应用无需用户注册或登录账号即可使用主要功能。

We do not actively collect personal identification information such as your name, phone number, email address, contacts, or precise location.

我们不会主动收集您的姓名、手机号码、电子邮箱、 通讯录或精确位置信息等个人身份信息。

Quiz records, scores, game levels, game progress, preferences, and similar application data are primarily used to provide application functionality and improve the gameplay experience.

用户在应用中产生的答题记录、得分、游戏关卡、 游戏进度以及相关设置，主要用于应用功能展示和游戏体验。

Such data is primarily stored locally on the user's device and is not actively uploaded to our servers.

相关数据主要保存在用户设备本地， 不会主动上传至我们的服务器。

2. Photos and Photo Selection / 照片与图片选择

Shake It allows users to select photos from their device in order to use the application's interactive photo effects.

《摇一摇 - 趣味图片》允许用户从设备中主动选择图片， 用于应用内的图片互动和视觉特效功能。

Photo access or photo selection is used only when the user chooses to use features that require selecting an image.

仅当用户主动使用需要选择图片的功能时， 应用才会使用系统提供的照片选择功能或相关照片权限。

Selected photos are primarily processed locally on the user's device. We do not upload selected photos to our servers as part of the main photo effect functionality.

用户选择的图片主要在设备本地进行处理。 在主要图片特效功能中，我们不会将用户选择的图片 上传至我们的服务器。

Important / 说明：
Users decide which photos to select and use within the application. / 用户自行决定选择和使用哪些图片。

3. Device Permissions / 设备权限

Depending on the features you use, certain applications may request access to device capabilities such as photo selection or motion-related functionality.

根据用户实际使用的功能，部分应用可能需要使用 图片选择或设备运动相关功能。

We only use device permissions when necessary to provide the corresponding feature.

我们仅会在实现相应功能所必需的情况下使用相关设备权限。

If future versions require additional permissions, we will explain their purpose and request authorization in accordance with the operating system requirements.

如果未来版本新增需要使用其他设备权限的功能， 我们会向您说明相关用途，并按照系统要求获得授权后使用。

4. Third-Party Services / 第三方服务

Our current applications do not use third-party advertising services to collect user information, and we do not sell users' personal information to third parties.

目前，上述应用不通过第三方广告服务收集用户信息， 我们也不会向第三方出售用户的个人信息。

If future versions integrate analytics, advertising, cloud services, or other third-party services, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

如果未来接入第三方统计、广告、云服务或其他服务， 我们将根据实际功能及时更新本隐私政策， 并说明相关的数据处理情况。

5. Children's Privacy / 儿童隐私保护

We value the protection of children's and minors' personal information.

我们重视未成年人及儿童的个人信息保护。

Our applications do not actively require children or other minors to provide names, contact information, or other information that directly identifies them.

上述应用不会主动要求儿童或其他未成年人提交姓名、 联系方式或其他能够直接识别个人身份的信息。

If future application features involve the processing of children's personal information, appropriate protections will be implemented in accordance with applicable laws.

如未来应用功能发生变化并涉及未成年人个人信息处理， 我们将依据相关法律法规采取适当的保护措施。

6. Data Storage and Security / 数据存储与安全

We take reasonable measures to protect application-related data.

我们会采取合理的安全措施保护应用相关数据。

The main functionality of our current applications operates locally on the user's iPhone and Apple Watch.

目前上述应用的主要功能在用户的 iPhone 和 Apple Watch 设备本地运行。

Because our applications do not require users to submit personal identification information, we currently do not maintain a database specifically for storing users' personal identity information.

由于应用目前不要求用户提交个人身份信息， 因此我们目前不会建立专门用于存储用户个人身份信息的数据库。

7. Data Sharing and Sale / 数据共享与出售

We do not sell users' personal information.

我们不会出售用户的个人信息。

Within the scope of the current application features, we do not actively share users' personal information with other companies, organizations, or individuals.

在目前的应用功能范围内， 我们不会主动将用户的个人信息共享给 其他公司、组织或个人。
