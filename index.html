Brain Teasers
脑静急转弯-手表益智问答

Brain Teasers is a trivia and puzzle application designed to provide quick and entertaining brain challenges, especially for Apple Watch users.

《脑静急转弯-手表益智问答》是一款休闲益智问答应用， 主要面向 Apple Watch 用户，为用户提供轻松、有趣的脑力挑战。

Frequently Asked Questions / 常见问题

How do I start a quiz?

如何开始答题？

Open Brain Teasers on your Apple Watch and tap the start option to begin answering questions.

在 Apple Watch 上打开应用，然后点击开始按钮即可进入答题。

How is my score calculated?

分数是如何计算的？

Your score is based on your answers and progress within the quiz.

分数根据您的答题结果以及当前答题进度进行计算。

Does Brain Teasers require an Internet connection?

应用需要连接互联网吗？

The main quiz features can work locally on your device and do not require a continuous Internet connection.

主要答题功能可以在设备本地运行， 不需要持续连接互联网。

What should I do if the quiz does not work correctly?

如果答题功能出现异常怎么办？

Try closing and reopening the application. You can also restart your Apple Watch and make sure that your device is running a supported system version.

请尝试关闭并重新打开应用。 您也可以重新启动 Apple Watch， 并确认设备正在运行受支持的系统版本。

Bean Break Master
拆豆大师-手表游戏

Bean Break Master is a casual puzzle game designed for iPhone and Apple Watch.

《拆豆大师》是一款支持 iPhone 和 Apple Watch 的休闲益智游戏。

Players observe the bean layout in each level, complete puzzle objectives, and progress through different challenges.

玩家需要观察每个关卡中的豆子布局， 完成对应的拆豆目标并逐步挑战不同关卡。

Frequently Asked Questions / 常见问题

How do I start the game?

如何开始游戏？

Open Bean Break Master on your iPhone or Apple Watch and tap the start button to begin the game.

在 iPhone 或 Apple Watch 上打开《拆豆大师》， 点击开始按钮即可进入游戏。

How do I progress through levels?

如何进入下一关？

Complete the objective of the current puzzle to continue to the next level.

完成当前关卡规定的益智目标后， 即可继续进入下一关。

Is my game progress stored online?

游戏进度会上传到服务器吗？

Game progress is primarily stored locally on your device.

游戏进度主要保存在您的设备本地。

Does Bean Break Master require an Internet connection?

《拆豆大师》需要网络连接吗？

The main gameplay does not require a continuous Internet connection.

主要游戏功能不需要持续连接互联网。

Can I play Bean Break Master on Apple Watch?

可以在 Apple Watch 上游玩吗？

Yes. Bean Break Master supports Apple Watch and provides gameplay designed for the watch interface.

可以。《拆豆大师》支持 Apple Watch， 并针对手表界面提供相应的游戏体验。

Shake It
摇一摇 - 趣味图片

Shake It is a fun photo effects application designed for iPhone.

《摇一摇 - 趣味图片》是一款面向 iPhone 的趣味图片互动应用。

Users can select a photo and interact with selected areas of the image using device motion to create playful and dynamic visual effects.

用户可以选择设备中的图片，并选取图片中的特定区域， 通过移动或晃动 iPhone 产生有趣的动态视觉效果。

Frequently Asked Questions / 常见问题

How do I use Shake It?

如何使用《摇一摇》？

Open Shake It, select a photo from your device, choose the area you want to apply the effect to, and move or shake your iPhone to see the visual effect.

打开应用，从设备中选择一张图片， 选择需要应用效果的区域，然后移动或晃动 iPhone 即可查看动态效果。

Why does the app request access to my photos?

为什么应用需要访问照片？

Photo access is used only so that you can select images from your photo library for use within the application.

照片权限仅用于让您从系统照片库中选择图片， 以便在应用内使用。

Are my photos uploaded to a server?

我的照片会上传到服务器吗？

Photo processing is performed locally on your device. Your selected photos are not uploaded to our servers as part of the main photo effect features.

图片处理主要在您的设备本地完成。 在主要图片特效功能中， 您选择的照片不会上传到我们的服务器。

Does Shake It require an Internet connection?

《摇一摇》需要互联网连接吗？

The main photo effect features can be used locally and do not require a continuous Internet connection.

主要图片特效功能可以在本地使用， 不需要持续连接互联网。

What should I do if the motion effect does not respond?

如果晃动效果没有反应怎么办？

Make sure you have selected an effect area correctly. Then try moving or shaking your iPhone again. If the issue continues, close and reopen the application or restart your device.

请确认已经正确选择效果区域， 然后再次移动或晃动 iPhone。 如果问题仍然存在，请尝试重新打开应用或重新启动设备。

Can I choose a custom area of a photo?

可以自定义图片区域吗？

Yes. Shake It allows you to select or define an area of the photo where the interactive effect will be applied.

可以。您可以选择或自定义图片中的特定区域， 并将互动效果应用到该区域。

Troubleshooting / 故障排除

What should I do if an app is not working correctly?

如果应用无法正常运行怎么办？

Please make sure your iPhone and Apple Watch are running supported system versions. Try closing and reopening the app or restarting your device. If the problem continues, please contact us.

请确认您的 iPhone 和 Apple Watch 正在运行受支持的系统版本。 您可以尝试关闭并重新打开应用或重新启动设备。 如果问题仍然存在，请联系我们。

What should I do if the Apple Watch app does not open correctly?

Apple Watch 应用无法正常打开怎么办？

Make sure your Apple Watch is properly paired with your iPhone, the application is installed correctly, and both devices are running supported system versions.

请确认 Apple Watch 已正确与 iPhone 配对， 应用已经正确安装，并且两个设备均运行受支持的系统版本。

What should I do if the Apple Watch app is not installed?

Apple Watch 上没有安装应用怎么办？

Open the Watch app on your iPhone and check whether the application is available for installation on your Apple Watch.

请打开 iPhone 上的 Watch 应用， 检查该应用是否可以安装到您的 Apple Watch。

What should I do if the app closes unexpectedly?

应用意外退出怎么办？

Restart the application first. If the issue continues, restart your device and check whether a newer version of the application is available.

请先重新启动应用。 如果问题仍然存在，请重新启动设备， 并检查 App Store 中是否有更新版本。
